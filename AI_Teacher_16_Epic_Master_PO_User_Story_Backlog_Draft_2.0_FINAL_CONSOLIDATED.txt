### H.5 Security contract

- Auth required.
- Student identity server-derived from `students_v2.user_id`.

- Organization is server-derived.
- Active class enrollment required for student access where applicable.
- Attempt ownership enforced on every read/write.
- Cross-tenant and IDOR access rejected.
- Correct answers never exposed pre-submit.
- Submitted attempts immutable.
- Published/archived practice content immutable for MVP.
